5) Expression
==========================================

Note: without the parentheses the expression would be evaluated with
the wrong precedence:
10.5 + 2 * 3 / 45 - 3.5 = 7.133333333333333 (wrong)
The correct grouping is (10.5 + 2 * 3) / (45 - 3.5)
